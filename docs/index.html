The Gap

Selection prep runs on borrowed credibility.

Most candidates cobble together their training from generic fitness influencers who've never seen a PST scoresheet, or from decade-old PDFs passed around a unit with no structure or progression. There's no system built by someone in the pipeline, tracking the actual standards, in real time.

Problem 1

No real standard-tracking

Apps track reps and miles. They don't track you against the actual minimum, competitive, and max lines a board is scoring you on.

Problem 2

Generic programming

Selection demands strength, swim, ruck, and run capacity at once. Most programs are built for one discipline and bolt the rest on badly.

Problem 3

Zero proof-of-work

Anyone can sell a PDF. Almost nobody selling one is training to the same standard, on the same clock, right now.

The Program

A 12-week block, built the way it's actually trained.

Strength, conditioning, swim, and mental preparation, periodized together — not four separate apps stitched into one routine.

01 / Weeks 1–4

Base & Standard Check

Establish current PST numbers against the minimum, competitive, and max standard lines. Build aerobic base and foundational strength while correcting the biggest single limiter first.

02 / Weeks 5–8

Load & Capacity

Increase strength and swim/run capacity concurrently. Weekly log entries track trend lines, not just single workouts — so drift gets caught early.

03 / Weeks 9–12

Peak & Board-Ready

Taper volume, sharpen test-day pacing, and lock in the mental preparation framework for the day the numbers actually count.
